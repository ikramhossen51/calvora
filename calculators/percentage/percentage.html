How to Calculate a Percentage

To calculate a percentage of a number, convert the percentage into a decimal and multiply it by the number.

Percentage (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

When Should You Use a Percentage Calculator?

A percentage calculator can help when calculating discounts, comparing values, understanding test scores, checking business figures, calculating tips, or finding what percentage one number represents of another.

For example, 20% of 150 is 30. The calculator makes this type of percentage calculation fast and easy without requiring manual multiplication or division.

Percentage Calculator FAQ

What is a percentage calculator?

A percentage calculator helps you calculate percentages, percentage values, and percentage-based calculations quickly.

How do you calculate a percentage?

Divide the part by the whole and multiply the result by 100 to find the percentage.

How do you calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide the difference by the original value, and multiply by 100.

How do you calculate percentage decrease?

Subtract the new value from the original value, divide the difference by the original value, and multiply by 100.
