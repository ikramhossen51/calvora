How to Calculate a Percentage

A percentage represents a part of a whole out of 100. To calculate a percentage, divide the part by the whole and multiply the result by 100.

Percentage (Part ÷ Whole) × 100
Percentage of a Number (Percentage ÷ 100) × Number

Percentage Calculator Example

For example, to calculate 20% of 150, divide 20 by 100 and multiply the result by 150. The answer is 30.

Example 20% × 150 = 30
