Margin Calculator

A margin calculator helps you determine profit, profit margin, and markup using the cost price and selling price of a product or service. It can be useful for businesses, retailers, ecommerce sellers, freelancers, and anyone who wants to understand the profitability of a sale.

How to Calculate Profit Margin

To calculate profit margin, first subtract the cost price from the selling price. This gives you the profit. Next, divide the profit by the selling price and multiply by 100.

Profit Selling Price − Cost Price
Profit Margin (Profit ÷ Selling Price) × 100
Markup (Profit ÷ Cost Price) × 100

Margin vs. Markup

Margin and markup are different ways of measuring profitability. Profit margin uses the selling price as the base, while markup uses the original cost price as the base. Understanding both metrics can help with pricing and business decisions.

When Should You Use a Margin Calculator?

Use a margin calculator when you want to check the profitability of a product, evaluate a selling price, compare pricing strategies, or understand how much profit remains after costs. It can be useful for ecommerce stores, small businesses, retailers, freelancers, and service providers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a profit margin?

Profit margin is the percentage of the selling price that remains as profit after the cost has been deducted.

How do you calculate profit margin?

Subtract the cost from the selling price to find the profit. Then divide the profit by the selling price and multiply by 100.

What is markup?

Markup measures profit relative to the original cost. Divide profit by cost and multiply by 100 to calculate markup.

What is the difference between margin and markup?

Margin uses the selling price as the base, while markup uses the cost price as the base.
