How to Calculate Profit Margin

First, subtract the cost price from the selling price to find the profit. Then divide the profit by the selling price and multiply by 100 to calculate the profit margin.

Profit Selling Price − Cost Price
Profit Margin (Profit ÷ Selling Price) × 100
Markup (Profit ÷ Cost Price) × 100

Margin vs. Markup

Profit margin and markup are related but they are not the same. Profit margin measures profit as a percentage of the selling price, while markup measures profit as a percentage of the original cost. Understanding both numbers can help you make better pricing and business decisions.

When Should You Use a Margin Calculator?

A margin calculator can help you check the profitability of a product, evaluate a selling price, compare different pricing strategies, estimate profit, and understand how much of your selling price remains after costs.

It can be useful for ecommerce businesses, online stores, retailers, wholesalers, freelancers, service providers, entrepreneurs, and small businesses.

Margin Calculator FAQ

What is a profit margin?

Profit margin is the percentage of the selling price that remains as profit after the cost has been deducted.

How do you calculate profit margin?

Subtract the cost from the selling price to find the profit. Then divide the profit by the selling price and multiply the result by 100.

What is markup?

Markup measures profit relative to the original cost. Divide the profit by the cost price and multiply by 100 to calculate markup.

What is the difference between margin and markup?

Profit margin uses the selling price as the base, while markup uses the original cost price as the base.

Can a margin calculator calculate a loss?

Yes. If the selling price is lower than the cost price, the calculator can show a negative profit or loss and a negative margin.
