Margin Calculator

A margin calculator helps you quickly determine your profit, profit margin, and markup based on the cost of a product and its selling price. It is useful for businesses, retailers, ecommerce sellers, freelancers, and anyone who wants to understand the profitability of a sale.

How to Calculate Profit Margin

To calculate profit margin, first subtract the cost price from the selling price. This gives you the profit. Then divide the profit by the selling price and multiply by 100.

Profit Selling Price − Cost Price
Profit Margin (Profit ÷ Selling Price) × 100
Markup (Profit ÷ Cost Price) × 100

Margin vs. Markup

Margin and markup are related, but they are not the same. Profit margin measures profit as a percentage of the selling price, while markup measures profit as a percentage of the original cost. Understanding both numbers can help you make better pricing decisions.

When Should You Use a Margin Calculator?

Use a margin calculator when you want to check the profitability of a product, compare pricing strategies, estimate profit from a sale, or understand how much of your selling price remains after costs. The calculator can be useful for ecommerce stores, small businesses, retailers, freelancers, and service providers.

Margin Calculator FAQ

What is a profit margin?

Profit margin is the percentage of the selling price that remains as profit after subtracting the cost.

How do I calculate profit margin?

Subtract the cost from the selling price to find profit. Then divide the profit by the selling price and multiply by 100.

What is the difference between margin and markup?

Margin is based on the selling price, while markup is based on the original cost price.

Can a margin be negative?

Yes. A negative margin occurs when the selling price is lower than the cost price, resulting in a loss.

Who can use a margin calculator?

Businesses, retailers, ecommerce sellers, freelancers, and anyone who wants to understand the profitability of a sale can use a margin calculator.
