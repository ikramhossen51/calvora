Margin Calculator – Calculate Profit Margin and Markup

Calvora's free Margin Calculator helps you calculate profit, profit margin, and markup using your cost price and selling price. It is useful for business owners, retailers, ecommerce sellers, freelancers, and anyone who wants to understand the profitability of a sale.

Enter your cost price and selling price to quickly see how much profit you make, what percentage of the selling price is profit, and how much your selling price is marked up from the original cost.

How to Calculate Profit Margin

First, subtract the cost price from the selling price to calculate your profit. Then divide the profit by the selling price and multiply by 100 to find your profit margin.

Profit Selling Price − Cost Price
Profit Margin (Profit ÷ Selling Price) × 100
Markup (Profit ÷ Cost Price) × 100

Margin vs. Markup

Profit margin and markup are related but they measure profit differently. Margin uses the selling price as the base, while markup uses the cost price as the base. Knowing both can help you make better pricing decisions.

When Should You Use a Margin Calculator?

A margin calculator is useful when pricing products, checking business profitability, comparing selling prices, evaluating ecommerce products, or determining whether a sale provides enough profit.

Margin Calculator FAQ

What is a profit margin?

Profit margin is the percentage of the selling price that remains as profit after the cost is deducted.

How do I calculate profit margin?

Subtract the cost from the selling price, divide the profit by the selling price, and multiply by 100.

What is markup?

Markup is the profit expressed as a percentage of the original cost price.

What is the difference between margin and markup?

Margin uses selling price as the base, while markup uses cost price as the base.
