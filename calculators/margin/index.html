Margin Calculator – Calculate Profit Margin and Markup

Use this free online margin calculator to calculate profit, profit margin, and markup from your cost price and selling price. The tool is useful for business owners, retailers, ecommerce sellers, freelancers, and anyone who wants to understand the profitability of a product or service.

Enter your cost and selling price to instantly see how much profit you make, your profit margin percentage, and your markup percentage. This makes it easier to check pricing and understand the difference between margin and markup.

Margin Calculator Formula

Profit margin is calculated using the profit divided by the selling price. Markup is calculated using the profit divided by the original cost price.

Profit Selling Price − Cost Price
Profit Margin (Profit ÷ Selling Price) × 100
Markup (Profit ÷ Cost Price) × 100

Margin vs. Markup

Margin and markup both describe profitability, but they use different reference values. Profit margin measures profit as a percentage of the selling price, while markup measures profit as a percentage of the cost price.

You can use a margin calculator whenever you need to quickly determine how profitable a sale is. It can be useful for online stores, retailers, small businesses, freelancers, service providers, and entrepreneurs.

Margin Calculator FAQ

What is a profit margin?

Profit margin is the percentage of the selling price that remains as profit after the cost has been deducted.

How do I calculate profit margin?

Subtract the cost from the selling price to find profit. Then divide profit by selling price and multiply by 100.

What is markup?

Markup measures profit relative to the original cost price. It is calculated by dividing profit by cost and multiplying by 100.

What is the difference between margin and markup?

Margin uses the selling price as the base, while markup uses the cost price as the base.
