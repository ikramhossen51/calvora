What is a profit margin?

Profit margin shows how much of your selling price remains as profit after the cost of the product or service is deducted.

Margin Formula

Profit = Selling Price − Cost Price

Margin = Profit ÷ Selling Price × 100

Markup = Profit ÷ Cost Price × 100
