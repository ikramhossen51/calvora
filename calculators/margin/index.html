Margin Calculator – Calculate Profit Margin and Markup

Use Calvora's free Margin Calculator to calculate profit, profit margin, and markup from your cost price and selling price. This online margin calculator is useful for businesses, retailers, ecommerce sellers, freelancers, and service providers.

Simply enter your cost price and selling price to instantly see how much profit you make, your profit margin percentage, and your markup percentage.

How to Calculate Profit Margin

Profit is the difference between the selling price and the cost price. Profit margin shows profit as a percentage of the selling price.

Profit Selling Price − Cost Price
Profit Margin (Profit ÷ Selling Price) × 100
Markup (Profit ÷ Cost Price) × 100

When Should You Use a Margin Calculator?

A margin calculator can help you understand product profitability, check pricing decisions, compare selling prices, and determine how much profit remains after costs. It can be useful when pricing products for an online store, retail business, marketplace, or service business.

For example, if a product costs $40 and sells for $60, the profit is $20. The profit margin is based on the $60 selling price, while markup is based on the $40 cost price.

Margin Calculator FAQ

What is a margin calculator?

A margin calculator helps you calculate profit, profit margin, and markup from cost and selling price.

How do you calculate profit margin?

Subtract cost from selling price, divide the profit by selling price, and multiply by 100.

What is markup?

Markup is profit expressed as a percentage of the original cost price.

What is the difference between margin and markup?

Margin uses selling price as the base, while markup uses cost price as the base.
