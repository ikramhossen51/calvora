Margin Calculator Formula

The margin calculation starts by finding the difference between the selling price and the cost price. The resulting profit is then used to calculate profit margin and markup.

Profit Selling Price − Cost Price
Profit Margin (Profit ÷ Selling Price) × 100
Markup (Profit ÷ Cost Price) × 100

Margin vs. Markup

Margin and markup are not the same measurement. Profit margin shows profit as a percentage of the selling price, while markup shows profit as a percentage of the original cost. Knowing both numbers can help you understand pricing and profitability.

When to Use a Margin Calculator

A margin calculator can help when setting product prices, checking ecommerce profits, comparing selling prices, reviewing business pricing, or estimating the profitability of products and services.

Margin Calculator FAQ

What is a margin calculator?

A margin calculator calculates profit, profit margin, and markup using your cost price and selling price.

How do you calculate profit margin?

Subtract the cost price from the selling price to find profit. Then divide the profit by the selling price and multiply by 100.

What is markup?

Markup measures profit compared with the original cost price. It is calculated by dividing profit by cost price and multiplying by 100.

What is the difference between margin and markup?

Margin uses the selling price as the base, while markup uses the cost price as the base.
